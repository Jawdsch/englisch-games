Rules

1 · Read and place / 2 · Listen and place

  1. Read or listen to the instructions: Put the cat under the bed.
  2. Drag the things from the box at the bottom to the right place in the room.
  3. Press Check. Right on the first try = 2 ⭐, after a correction = 1 ⭐.

3 · Where is it?

  1. The things are already in the room. Answer the questions in full sentences: It is under the bed. – for more things: They are on the shelf.
  2. Right on the first try = 2 ⭐, on the second try = 1 ⭐.

The rounds get harder: first on, under, in – then next to, between, in front of, behind.
