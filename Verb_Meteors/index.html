Rules

  1. Irregular verbs fall from the sky. Write or tap the past simple before the meteor hits the ground.
  2. Right answer: the meteor explodes and you get points. Many right answers in a row = bonus points.
  3. Wrong answer: you lose 5 points, the meteor jumps down – and the whole game gets faster. Don't guess!
  4. When you tap a wrong word, the buttons are locked for one second.
  5. A meteor hits the ground: you lose a heart ♥. No hearts left = game over.
  6. Every 4 right answers you go up one level. The meteors get faster.
  7. Every verb comes once before verbs come again. Verbs you get wrong come back soon.
  8. Typing: press Enter after every word. For be you can write was or were.
  9. At the end you see the verbs you should practise.
