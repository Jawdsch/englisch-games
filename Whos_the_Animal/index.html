Rules

  1. I am thinking of one of the 25 animals on the cards. Find out which one! (Every round has new animals.)
  2. Write yes/no questions in English. I only answer correct questions.
  3. Every question counts. A wrong question (for example Can it swims?) also counts – and I tell you the right form.
  4. Tap the animals that can't be the answer to cross them out.
  5. When you know it, ask: Is it a …? You can guess only after 3 questions. You have 3 guesses – then a new animal comes and you get no stars. A wrong guess counts as two questions.
