    Part 2 – write the family word

    Rules

    1. Read the clues, for example: Tom is Lisa’s brother.
    2. Tap a name, then tap the right box in the family tree. Tap a box again to take the name out.
    3. The pictures help: grandparents at the top, parents in the middle, children at the bottom. Lines with ♥ = married.
    4. Press Check the family. You only see how many names are right – not which ones.
    5. Solved on the first check = ⭐⭐⭐, second check = ⭐⭐, third check = ⭐, later = no stars. So think first – don’t just try!
    6. Part 2: write the missing family words (Mia is Peter’s niece.). Every right word = ⭐.
    7. Easy: mother, father, son, daughter, brother, sister, husband, wife, grandparents. Hard: also aunt, uncle, cousin, niece, nephew, grandson, granddaughter, sister-in-law, brother-in-law.

    You did it!
